What this check can — and can't — tell you

The subject's eight 3D bounding-box corners must fit within the lens frustum, including framing expansion. Required distance includes subject depth and off-centre placement. Backward travel stops at the first wall, floor or ceiling, with 0.25m clearance.

Wall / ceiling warnings mean the ideal frame at subject depth extends outside the room. In reality, opaque room surfaces appear in the shot — the camera never sees through walls. These are composition warnings, not automatic failures.

Rectilinear pinhole optics only: no furniture occlusion, focus breathing, distortion, stabilisation crop, portrait rotation or safety guarantee. This web room is rectangular; the Swift module accepts actual RoomPlan wall rectangles.

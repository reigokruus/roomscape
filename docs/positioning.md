# Positioning and similar apps

*Researched 6 November 2026 at the Film & Multimedia Tech Hackathon, IDA Hub Jõhvi.*

## What we're building
A **set-up assistant for real rooms**, for everyday creators, not film professionals:

1. **Scan** the real room with the iPhone's LiDAR (walls, windows, doors, furniture).
2. **Know your gear**: a simple inventory of what you actually own (phone, ring light, desk lamp, LED panel, a white sheet as a reflector).
3. **Pick a scene** (interview, vlog, product shot, dinner scene). The app suggests, in plain language, where to put the camera and each piece of *your* gear, using existing windows and lamps as light sources, plus a few creative scene ideas.

## Similar apps and how we differ

| App | What it does | How we differ |
|---|---|---|
| **Previs Pro** | 3D storyboarding and animatics for filmmakers. You build virtual sets from a library of props, actors and lights, and it exports storyboards and PDFs. It has AR placement on location, and its "AI Light Grade" re-lights *virtual* scenes for mood. Free for 10 projects, then $39.99 a month or $119.99 a year. | It doesn't scan the room, doesn't track your own gear and doesn't suggest placement. It's a **pro storyboarding tool**, and we're a **set-up assistant for real rooms**. |
| **WATT-IF** (beta) | The closest match. It turns a LiDAR iPhone scan into a 3D set, places virtual cinema lights, estimates lux and key-to-fill ratios, offers portrait presets and an AI critique, and exports set plan PDFs. $19–99 a month. | It's aimed at DPs and gaffers with pro fixtures, and you place things by hand. We start from the user's own everyday gear and *suggest* the setup. |
| **Polycam** | A general 3D capture app using LiDAR, photogrammetry and Gaussian splats, with a room and floor-plan mode and exports to 3D and CAD formats. Freemium with a paid subscription. | It captures and measures spaces but doesn't plan a shoot. It has no gear inventory and no light or camera suggestions. |
| **Scaniverse** | A free 3D scanning app (from Niantic) that makes LiDAR meshes and Gaussian splats on the phone itself. | Like Polycam, it captures the space but doesn't plan anything in it. Either could be a capture front-end, but neither offers set-up guidance. |
| **Photo Studio AR** | AR previs with virtual models, lights and props for photographers. | It doesn't scan the room, you place everything manually, and it uses no real gear. |
| **Shot Designer, Cine Designer, Artemis** | Manual set diagrams, 3D previs and director's viewfinders. | They need pro know-how and offer no suggestions. |
| **Illuma AI** | Analyses the light in a photo you've already taken. | It analyses after the fact instead of planning ahead. |

**Our gap:** none of these combines *scan the real room* + *your own gear* + *automatic, plain-language placement suggestions* for non-professionals.
